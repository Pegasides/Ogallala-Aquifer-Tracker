AI Data Centers

This is the deep-dive destination for the infrastructure story: where facilities are operating, where construction is underway, what is proposed, and how each project sits in relation to the Ogallala Aquifer, communities, and public groundwater information.

The page is designed to hold the facility graphics already prepared for the Tracker while keeping the public-facing map simple enough to understand at a glance.

Evidence rule: a data center appearing on the map is geographic and development-status context. It is not, by itself, proof that the facility caused a nearby groundwater decline. Facility water claims, permit records, monitoring data, and independent measurements must remain clearly separated.

Operational

Under construction

Projects with active construction evidence. Construction status does not imply that full operations have begun.

Proposed

Announced, planned, or proposed projects that have not yet reached the operational category.

What a subscriber should be able to answer

The Data Centers button is for the deeper investigation; Substack can point readers here when a project or groundwater development needs context.

Where is it?Show the facility on the same aquifer map readers already know, alongside the appropriate community reference points.
What is its real status?Separate operational, under construction, and proposed projects. Put the evidence date beside the status.
What do we actually know about water?Separate permits, company statements, modeled context, monitoring-well observations, and independent findings instead of blending them together.
