Grand Island Monitoring Well

Merrick County, Nebraska · High Plains Aquifer · USGS / Central Platte Natural Resources District

Grand Island water-table history

Well depth: 30 feet · Measured quantity: depth to water below land surface. A larger value means the water table is farther underground.

Complete available record: the selected annual measurement changed from 8.19 feet below land surface in 1999 to 7.85 feet in 2026—0.34 feet shallower overall. The record fluctuates seasonally but does not show the long-term systemic decline documented at Alliance.

Source: USGS Water Data for the Nation · Site 405227098165601

Monitoring well 11N 8W31CCCA1: 30-foot physical depth · High Plains aquifer · constructed in 1999 · annual approved static measurement selected nearest March 20 when available. Because this well began in 1999, a 40-year graph cannot be supported; no earlier values are estimated.
