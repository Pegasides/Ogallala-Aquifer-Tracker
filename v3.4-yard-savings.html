Step 3 · How it works

Simple, visible assumptions

  • Water volume: One inch of water across 1,000 sq. ft. is approximately 623 gallons. The planning range uses 1.0–1.5 inches per week and 4.33 weeks per month.
  • Alliance water rates: Urban residential usage rates currently range from $1.557 to $2.706 per 100 cubic feet; 100 cubic feet equals 748 gallons. The calculator does not count the fixed meter charge as savings.
  • Actual Bill mode: The seasonal water amount is the summer water-only bill minus the winter water-only bill. Savings are adjusted for the share of lawn converted and the selected irrigation reduction.
  • Time: The secondary time estimate assumes four mowings per active month and about 20 minutes per 1,000 sq. ft. It does not depend on mower type and is not included in dollar savings.
