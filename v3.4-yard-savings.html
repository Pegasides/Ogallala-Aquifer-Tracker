Definition

Scenario B (The Real-World Model)

In Alliance, Nebraska, a typical household easily fills up the city's lower utility tier (Block 1) just through normal indoor living like showers, laundry, and dishes. Under Scenario B, because your indoor life already consumes that baseline tier, 100% of your outdoor lawn watering is added on top and billed at the elevated Block 2 rate of $1.759 per CCF.

What the transition can save

Because established native Nebraska grasses can survive almost entirely on natural rainfall and do not require chemical treatments, switching away from turf grass wipes out your fully loaded Scenario B baseline.

When you scale this transition across different yard sizes, your net seasonal savings—calculated by subtracting the minimal native grass upkeep of $30–$120 from the fully loaded turf costs above—multiply significantly:
