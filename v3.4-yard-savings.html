3 · Methodology

What the calculator is doing

  • Water volume: 1 inch across 1,000 sq. ft. ≈ 623 gallons. Monthly planning volume uses 4.33 weeks/month and a 1.0–1.5 in./week summer benchmark, then applies your chosen irrigation-reduction percentage.
  • Alliance water charges: urban residential usage rates are $1.557, $1.759, $2.165 and $2.706 per 100 cubic feet. One hundred cubic feet is 748 gallons. The fixed meter service charge is not treated as avoidable.
  • Alliance residential electricity: the current City rate sheet lists $0.1159/kWh for the first 600 kWh and for summer excess kWh. The standard electric-mower estimate uses the mower kW, mowing time and mowings/month that you can edit above.
  • Actual Bill mode: seasonal water signal = max(0, summer water-only bill − winter water-only bill). Estimated water-bill savings = seasonal signal × converted-lawn share × irrigation-reduction percentage. This is a household estimate, not a guarantee that every summer/winter difference is caused by lawn irrigation.
  • Annual values: monthly in-season cash savings × the number of active watering/maintenance months you choose. Default = 6 months.
  • Time: shown separately from cash. If you enter a dollar value for your time, the calculator reports that optional value separately rather than hiding it inside bill savings.
