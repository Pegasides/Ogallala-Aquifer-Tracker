What lies beneath

One aquifer. Eight states. Different local realities.

The Ogallala is not a single bathtub that rises and falls evenly. Conditions vary across the High Plains, which is why Version 3.1 separates the story into Northern, Central, and Southern basin views and adds community reference points for geographic orientation.

The goal is simple: make groundwater change understandable without pretending the science is simpler than it is.

Everything is connected

Groundwater is not a story about water alone.

Version 3.1 is organized around connections a reader can actually feel: what happens beneath a farm or town, what changes around a community, and what choices above ground influence demand and resilience.

💧

Water quantity + quality

Levels, drawdown, monitoring wells, water quality, and the difference between basin-wide pressure and local conditions.

🏘️

People + place

Farmers, towns, rural homeowners, schools, property, infrastructure, and the local decisions that shape groundwater use.

🌾

Land + habitat + demand

Irrigation, water-wise yards, pollinators, industry, digital infrastructure, and practical ways to reduce unnecessary pressure.
