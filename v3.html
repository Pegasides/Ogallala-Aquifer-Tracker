What lies beneath

One aquifer. Eight states. Different local realities.

The Ogallala is not a single bathtub that rises and falls evenly. Conditions vary across the High Plains, which is why Version 3.2 separates the story into Northern, Central, and Southern basin views and adds community reference points for geographic orientation.

The goal is simple: make groundwater change understandable without pretending the science is simpler than it is.
